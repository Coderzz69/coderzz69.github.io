Last updated 31 October 2026

What the app collects

During a trip:

  • the phone's motion sensors (accelerometer, gyroscope);
  • its GPS position, speed and heading;
  • the road problems it detects;
  • the settings you choose for the trip (vehicle type, how the phone is mounted).

The app never asks for your name, phone number, email, contacts, photos or microphone.

Location

Location is used during a trip, including when the app is closed or the screen is off. It places each road problem on the map and warns you before the ones ahead. Before a trip, the map uses location only while the app is open. You can turn location off in your phone's settings; trips then record without a map.

What stays on your phone

Every trip is saved on your phone; you can delete any trip in the Trips tab. With Settings › Share trips automatically turned off, trips stay only on the phone until you share them.

What is shared, and with whom

After each trip, unless you turned automatic sharing off in Settings, and whenever you share a trip yourself, the following are sent over an encrypted connection to the project's server (hosted on Supabase):

  • its recording (sensor readings and GPS track);
  • your phone's make and model;
  • a random upload ID.

The server finds road problems in the trip and adds them to the shared map. The map that everyone sees contains places only (a road problem and how many people felt it), never anyone's route.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers. The app has no ads and no analytics.

Maps

Maps are shown with Google Maps. To draw them, Google receives the area being viewed and technical data such as the phone's IP address, under Google's privacy policy. The places and trips drawn on the map are not sent to Google.

How long it is kept, and deleting it

Uploaded trips are kept for as long as they help build the map. To have everything uploaded from your phone deleted, write to the contact address above with the Upload ID shown under Settings › About & privacy. Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13.
